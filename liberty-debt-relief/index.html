FAQ

Common questions

What types of debt can Liberty Debt Relief help with? +
We help with unsecured debts such as credit cards, personal loans, medical bills, and private student loans. We cannot assist with secured debts (mortgages, auto loans), federal student loans, taxes, or utility bills.
Will debt settlement hurt my credit score? +
During the program, your credit score will likely decline because you stop making payments to creditors. However, once debts are settled, most clients see their credit recover over time. Many people find the long-term financial relief outweighs the temporary credit impact.
How much does Liberty Debt Relief charge? +
We charge zero upfront fees. Our program fee — typically 15–25% of enrolled debt — is only collected after a settlement has been successfully negotiated and you have approved it. You only pay when we deliver results.
How long does the program take? +
Most clients complete the program in 24–48 months, depending on the total enrolled debt and monthly deposit amount. Your dedicated account manager will give you a personalized timeline estimate during your free consultation.
What is the minimum debt amount to enroll? +
We typically work with clients who have a minimum of $10,000 in qualifying unsecured debt. This ensures our negotiation efforts can deliver meaningful savings relative to any costs or credit impacts.
Are there any tax implications? +
In some cases, forgiven debt over $600 may be reported to the IRS as income on a Form 1099-C. We recommend consulting a tax professional. However, many clients who are insolvent at the time of settlement may qualify for an IRS exclusion. We'll explain this fully during your consultation.
